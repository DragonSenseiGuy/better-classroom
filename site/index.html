Better Classroom

A personal, self-hosted view of your own Google Classroom. Announcements, assignments, materials and due dates from every course, in one inbox and one to-do list.

  • Read-only. It never posts, edits, grades or deletes anything.
  • Runs on your own computer. Your data stays in a local database.
  • Nothing is sent to a third party, sold, or used for advertising.
