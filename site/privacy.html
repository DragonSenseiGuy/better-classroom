Privacy Policy

Better Classroom · Effective 9 September 2026

Better Classroom is a personal, self-hosted application built and operated by Mahad Kalam ("I", "me"). It displays a user's own Google Classroom courses, announcements, coursework and due dates in one place. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and how you can remove it.

Who uses this app

The app is intended for individual use. Each person runs their own copy on their own computer and signs in with their own Google account. There is no central server operated by me that receives or stores anyone's data.

What data the app accesses

With your permission, the app reads the following from Google Classroom using read-only scopes:

  • The list of courses you are enrolled in, including course names and teachers.
  • Announcements, coursework, materials and topics posted in those courses.
  • Your own submission status and grades for coursework.
  • Your basic profile: name, email address and profile photo.

The app cannot post, edit, grade, delete or otherwise change anything in Google Classroom. It never accesses other students' submissions or grades.

How the data is used

Data is used solely to show you your own Classroom content inside the app: an inbox of recent posts, a to-do list of upcoming work, and course pages. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or training any model, and it is not sold.

Where the data is stored

All data is stored in a local database file on the computer where you run the app. Profile photos may be cached locally so they display quickly. Nothing is uploaded to me or to any third party. The only external service the app communicates with is Google's own APIs, in order to fetch your data.

Sharing

Your Google user data is not shared with, transferred to, or disclosed to anyone. It is not shared with third parties for any purpose.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Better Classroom's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your computer until you delete it. To remove it, delete the app's database file (by default data/classroom.sqlite) and any cached avatar files. To revoke the app's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Better Classroom. Revoking access stops all further data retrieval immediately.

Security

OAuth tokens are stored locally alongside the database and are never transmitted anywhere other than to Google. The app runs on localhost and is not exposed to the internet unless you choose to host it yourself.

Children

The app may be used by students, including those under 18, at their own choice and with their school account. No data is collected by me from any user of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
